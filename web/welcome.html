TunerBox

    Welcome to TunerBox

    Let's get you watching. A few quick questions: your area for the TV guide, where your channels come from, your own movies and shows, recordings, and a phone as the remote.

    Each step can be skipped, and everything can be changed later in Setup.

    Where do you watch?

    Your ZIP or postal code finds the TV providers near you. TunerBox then uses that provider's channel numbers and local listings, so the guide matches the one where you live. US and Canada.

    Where do your channels come from?

    Add an antenna tuner on your network, an M3U playlist, or an Xtream login from your provider. You can add more than one.

      Your own movies and shows

      Point TunerBox at a folder of movies or TV shows, on a NAS or on this computer, and they show up in On Demand. TunerBox only reads them; it never changes your files.

        Record shows?

        With recordings on, you can record a show or a whole series from the guide and watch it later. Off leaves Record and Recordings out of every screen.

        Use your phone as the remote

        A paired phone can change channels, browse the guide and search on any TunerBox screen. It has to be on the same Wi-Fi.
