Local channels

Turn folders of videos into TV channels that play around the clock, with their own guide listings. Folders can be on this PC or on the network (\\server\share\TV\Seinfeld). Tuning in joins whatever is "on" at the moment, like real TV. Episode names are read from file names such as Show - S01E02 - Title.mkv.

New local channel

Files are read in the background; long libraries on a network share take a while the first time.

Every file is converted to the same format while it plays (H.264, set the size under Settings), so mixed libraries work, at the cost of some CPU on this PC.

Local guide (Gracenote)

Pick your TV provider (cable, satellite or antenna) and TunerBox uses its channel numbers and local listings, so the numbers match the TV guide where you live. US and Canada.

Online guides (XMLTV)

Used for channels outside your lineup. One URL (or file path, .xml or .xml.gz) per line. Guide links found in your playlists and Xtream logins are added automatically.
